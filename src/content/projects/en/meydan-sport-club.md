**Meydan Sport Club** is a gym in Ortaçeşme, Beykoz, with a mixed gym and a
separate women-only gym. We designed, built and launched its website.

## What the gym needed

Someone choosing a gym first looks at where it is, what it costs, when it's
open and which classes it runs. Many also want to know whether there's a
separate gym for women. If they can't find this online, they either pick up
the phone or look at another gym whose details are easier to find.

Meydan Sport Club wanted a site that answers all of this in detail. The site
also had to be easy to find in searches and give the gym a tidy, professional
face online.

## Today

The site is live at [meydansportclub.com](https://www.meydansportclub.com/).
Anyone curious about the gym can see the prices, the class timetable, the
trainers and the opening hours in one place. Once they've made up their mind,
they can call the gym or message it on WhatsApp in one tap from their phone.
